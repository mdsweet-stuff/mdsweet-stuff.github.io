FliteMode Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

FliteMode is a collection of offline games for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: we don't collect anything about you ourselves. The app shows ads from Google, and Google collects some information to do that. If you buy Remove ads, RevenueCat keeps a record of the purchase so it can be restored.

What we collect

Nothing about you. FliteMode has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. We don't receive or store any information about you or how you play.

What stays on your phone

Your saved games, scores and stats are stored only on your device so you can carry on where you left off. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them, and you can clear any game's stats from its Stats page.

Ads

FliteMode shows full-screen ads between games, and offers optional ads you can watch for a hint or a rescue in some games. They're provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device information, such as model, operating system and settings
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • how you interact with ads
  • your device's advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking (see below)

Google uses this to choose and deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and measure performance. Google's use of this information is covered by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices

  • Personalised ads (UK and EEA): when you first open the app you'll be asked whether Google may use your data to personalise ads. You can change your answer at any time from Privacy settings at the bottom of the FliteMode home screen.
  • Tracking (iPhone): iOS asks whether FliteMode may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • If you decline, you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your data.

Remove ads

You can remove the ads with a one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details, name or email.

The purchase is recorded by RevenueCat, which we use to check it's genuine and to restore it if you reinstall the app or use another device. RevenueCat stores your purchase history against a random ID created by the app. That ID isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID, and isn't used for advertising or tracking.

Once you've bought it, the app no longer requests ads, so Google receives nothing from FliteMode.

Children

FliteMode is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
