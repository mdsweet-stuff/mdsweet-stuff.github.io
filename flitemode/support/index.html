Common questions

Where are my saved games and stats?

On your phone only. FliteMode has no accounts, so saves and stats aren't backed up anywhere else and don't carry over to a new phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I reset a game's stats?

Open the game, tap Stats, then Reset stats.

Does FliteMode need the internet?

No. Every game works offline. Ads only load when you're connected.

How often are ads shown?

Never during a game. A full-screen ad may appear after you finish a game, at most once every three games and no more than once every three minutes.

How do I change my ad and tracking choices?

Tap Privacy settings at the bottom of the home screen (UK and EEA). To stop tracking on iPhone, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

The daily WotWord says I've already played

Each daily word can be played once, and a new one arrives at midnight on your phone's clock. Unlimited mode is there in between.
